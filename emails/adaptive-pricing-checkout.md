### Getting Started

1. Go to **Settings > Payment Methods** and connect the crossborder payment methods you want to support. (Partner review takes 3-5 business days.)
2. Go to **Settings > Checkout Customisation > Adaptive Pricing** and turn on **Enable Adaptive Pricing**.
3. Save - all hosted checkout sessions will activate adaptive pricing for international customers automatically.

### Key Benefits

- **Better checkout conversion** - customers pay less at a better fx rate, using a payment method and in a currency they recognize
- **Auto-detects location** - shows the amount in the customer's local currency automatically
- **Cross-border payment methods surfaces automatically** - relevant payment methods appear to your customers (e.g. DuitNow, PayNow, QRPh, QRIS, VietQR and many more). See available methods in your HitPay Dashboard > Payment Methods
- **You save on transaction fees** - when your international customer selects a non-card payment method, it usually costs you less per transaction
- **You get paid the same way** - payouts always land in your home currency
