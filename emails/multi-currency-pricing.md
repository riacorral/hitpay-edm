---

### How to Set It Up

1. Go to **Point of Sale > Products** on your dashboard
2. Open an existing product or create a new one
3. Under **Pricing**, click **Add a price by currency**
4. Select the currency and enter your price
5. Repeat for each currency, then **Save**
