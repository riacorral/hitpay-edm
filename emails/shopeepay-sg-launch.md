## More ways for your customers to pay

Great news! You can now accept ShopeePay as a payment method on your online store via HitPay.

With ShopeePay enabled, your customers get more flexible ways to pay at checkout, including:

- **ShopeePay Wallet** for quick, familiar payments
- **MariBank** for direct bank transfers
- **SPayLater** for flexible instalment payments*

---

## Why enable ShopeePay?

- **Increase conversion** with more payment options at checkout
- **Reach millions** of ShopeePay users across Singapore
- **Seamless experience** with a trusted, familiar checkout flow

---

## How to enable ShopeePay

It takes less than a minute to get started.

### Step 1: Log in to your HitPay dashboard

![HitPay login page](file:///Users/riacorral/Documents/HitPay-Claude/Outputs/drafts/EDM-260707-ShopeePay/step1-login.png){width=250}

### Step 2: Select "Plugins Integration" from the left menu

![Plugins Integration in sidebar](file:///Users/riacorral/Documents/HitPay-Claude/Outputs/drafts/EDM-260707-ShopeePay/step2-plugins.png){width=250}

### Step 3: Select "Shopify Payments App" under Edit Integration

![Edit Integration dropdown](file:///Users/riacorral/Documents/HitPay-Claude/Outputs/drafts/EDM-260707-ShopeePay/step3-edit-integration.png){width=250}

### Step 4: Tick "ShopeePay" under payment methods

![ShopeePay checkbox ticked](file:///Users/riacorral/Documents/HitPay-Claude/Outputs/drafts/EDM-260707-ShopeePay/step4-shopeepay-tick.png){width=250}

### Step 5: Click "Update" and you're done

![Click Update button](file:///Users/riacorral/Documents/HitPay-Claude/Outputs/drafts/EDM-260707-ShopeePay/step5-update.png){width=400}

---
