## 3 New Checkout Upgrades, Live Now

Hi {firstName},

Checking in - have you had a chance to check out your new checkout page yet?

Along with the redesign, we've shipped two more customization options, plus a smarter upgrade for links you share directly with customers. ✨

---

### 1. Customizable branding
Choose to display either your logo and business name, or your logo only, on the checkout page.

![Customizable branding display options](img/checkout-branding-display.png)

Go to **Settings > Branding > Checkout Customization** to switch it.

---

### 2. Payment methods alignment

Choose whether payment methods on your checkout page are displayed horizontally or vertically - whichever fits your store's layout best.

::: image-row
img/checkout-payment-methods-horizontal.png | Horizontal payment method layout
img/checkout-payment-methods-vertical.png | Vertical payment method layout
:::

Go to **Settings > Branding > Checkout Customization** to switch it.

---

### 3. Rich link previews
Checkout links now generate previews when shared on platforms such as WhatsApp, Telegram, and Slack - helping build customer trust and improve conversion.

![Rich link previews on WhatsApp, Telegram and Slack](img/checkout-rich-link-previews.png)
