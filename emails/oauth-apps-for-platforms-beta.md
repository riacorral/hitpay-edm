Connecting a merchant today means digging up their API key and pasting it into your product - clunky, and risky if that secret leaks.

**OAuth Apps for Platforms** swaps that for a "Connect your HitPay account" button. One approval gets you a scoped token - no raw keys - revocable anytime from their dashboard.

---

### Set it up now

1. Go to **Payments > Others > Developers**
2. Click the **App Builder** tab
3. Click **+ New App**
4. Name your app, then copy your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**

![Find it under Developers → App Builder in your HitPay dashboard](img/oauth-app-builder-dashboard.png)

[Try Creating an OAuth App Now](https://dashboard.hit-pay.com?utm_source=email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=platforms-oauth&utm_content=1-try-creating-oauth-app){.cta}

---

### How it works

1. Merchant clicks "Connect your HitPay account"
2. Merchant sees a consent screen and approves
3. Your platform receives scoped OAuth tokens
4. Merchant can revoke access anytime from their dashboard

---

This is a **Beta**. Today, OAuth tokens cover Payment Requests, Charges, and Refunds - we're adding more API scopes soon. Early platforms get a head start and a direct line to shape what we build next.
