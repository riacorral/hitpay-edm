## How to Top Up Your Balance

1. Navigate to **Bank Payouts**
2. Click on **HitPay Balance**
3. Click on **"Top up balance"**
4. Enter the top-up amount and click **"Next"**
5. Select the payment method you'd like to top up with
6. Make the payment — you'll get a success message once it's done

Your balance is added automatically to your **Reserve** sub-balance or can be transferred from your **Available Balance**, which is used to deduct refund amounts for non-card payments. If it doesn't update right away, just refresh the page.
